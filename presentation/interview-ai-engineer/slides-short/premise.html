The premise

A model that is sometimes wrong, writing into a calendar two parents act on.

Not a bad answer in a chat window. An action nobody re-reads the source for.

1 / 13

This is the frame for everything that follows, so land it properly and then move fast. The system reads school WhatsApp groups and teacher email, pulls out the real events, and syncs them to the family calendar. Hebrew and English, forwarded flyers, cancellations. But the difficulty is not reading Hebrew. It is that the output is acted on. If a chatbot is wrong, a human reads it and shrugs. If this is wrong, an event lands on a calendar two parents coordinate around, and nobody goes back to the source message. That property — output that becomes an action — is exactly what makes a bank nervous about GenAI. Every control in the next ten slides exists because of it.
