## Which to use

**Short** — for presenting live. One headline per slide and a single
supporting line; everything else is in the speaker notes. The slide does not
compete with you, and nobody in the room reads ahead.

**Long** — for a 25-minute slot where the evidence should be visible, or for
sending ahead to be read without you in the room. Tables, numbers and the
reasoning on the slide itself.

They make the same argument in the same order. The short one assumes you are
there to make it.
